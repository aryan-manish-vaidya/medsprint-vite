Phase 01 · Aug 2026 IEEE ADYPU
the brief

Healthcare doesn't wait. Neither should the fix.

Hack-X puts engineering talent against a clock and a chart. Over 36 uninterrupted hours, teams design and build working solutions to real healthcare and clinical challenges — diagnostics, patient access, hospital workflows, health data, assistive tech, and more.

It's organized by Ajeenkya D Y Patil University and the IEEE ADYPU Student Branch, run as the first of a two-phase format that carries top teams all the way to IIT Bombay.

No prior healthcare background required — just a team, a problem worth solving, and 36 hours to prove it.

the format

Two phases. One shot at IIT Bombay.

Hack-X runs as a qualifier-to-final pipeline — build at ADYPU, and if you're in the top 3, build again on the national stage.

STAGE01
Qualifier · Ajeenkya D Y Patil University

Phase 01 — The ADYPU Sprint

A 36-hour on-site build at ADYPU, Pune. Teams tackle a medical/healthcare problem statement end-to-end — from diagnosis to working demo — in front of judges from academia and industry.

↓  top 3 teams advance
STAGE02
Finals · IIT Bombay

Phase 02 — The IIT Bombay Finals

The top 3 teams from Phase 01 travel to IIT Bombay to compete at the national stage. Format, dates, and finals problem statements will be announced closer to Phase 01's conclusion.
